I'm a Program Manager at Pratham Education Foundation with 5+ years in education and the social sector, leading a state-level partnership with the Tribal Development Department (Government of Maharashtra) across 500 tribal residential ashram schools. My work spans everyone from Grade 1 students learning to decode their first word to 29 Project Officers (17 IAS) aligning policy and reporting.

Most of what I do is unglamorous: reviewing telemetry data, travelling to remote ashramshalas across Nashik, Thane, Nagpur, and Amravati, troubleshooting implementation failures, and holding honest pedagogical conversations with headmasters and wardens until learning genuinely shifts. I also build practical EdTech — including an offline-first AI student tracking platform and interactive FLN games.

Before this I led multi-state STEM programs at Gurushala and American India Foundation, and directed 100+ street plays with Manthan Theatre Foundation. Educated at Kurukshetra University with both a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) and an M.A. in Hindi Language & Literature with Distinction — a combination of scientific inquiry and linguistic empathy that proves remarkably useful in the field.
